Gameplay & cinematic sequencing

Built a publish/subscribe system on top of Lyra's Gameplay Message Subsystem. Designers connect cutscenes, gameplay interactions, and player-control transitions by matching Gameplay Tags.

For example, interacting with a chair triggers a cutscene where Fret retrieves his guitar. Control shifts to guitar gameplay, followed by additional cinematics with various interactive gameplay sequences in between, before returning control to Fret. Designers can configure the entire sequence without writing code.

Selected puzzle implementations

Two of the puzzles I implemented from start to finish, including player interactions, puzzle logic, completion, configurable parameters, and audio hooks.

Both puzzles expose settings for designers to customize behavior and audio hooks for sound designers to connect sound effects.

Other contributions

Built data-driven tools with Gameplay Tags and Data Assets, implemented supporting AI behaviors, and worked on custom shaders, materials, and particle effects.
