01 / GAMEPLAY ARCHITECTURE

Event-Driven Gameplay & Cinematic Sequencing

Delivered a reusable system that allowed designers to construct complex playable sequences simply by matching Gameplay Tags.

Built a gameplay orchestration framework leveraging Lyra's tag-based publish/subscribe architecture. Individual gameplay systems could broadcast completion events and subscribe to events from other systems without requiring direct dependencies.

Designers could author cinematics in Unreal Engine and configure matching trigger and completion tags to chain together cutscenes, interactive gameplay, and transitions in player control. New sequences could be assembled from existing components without additional gameplay programming.

Example of a complete gameplay experience assembled from independent gameplay and cinematic components.

How the components communicate

Each component broadcasts an event when it completes. The next component subscribes to the matching Gameplay Tag and activates when that event is received. Designers connect the stages by configuring the tags.

Cinematic completion — broadcasts Cinematic.GuitarPickup.Completed
Guitar gameplay — listens for Cinematic.GuitarPickup.Completed

Illustrative Gameplay Tag names.

The result was a flexible, designer-driven workflow that kept cinematic playback, gameplay interactions, and player-control transitions decoupled while enabling the team to create complex scripted experiences.
